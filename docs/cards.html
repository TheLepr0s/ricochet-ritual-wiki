Rarity is a draw weight, not a power ranking. A legendary is not strictly better than a rare — it is rarer, which usually means it changes how a build works rather than how big its numbers are. Cards are removed from the draw entirely when they cannot help you: at their stack cap, missing a prerequisite, or conflicting with something you already own. Being offered a card that does nothing is worse than being offered nothing.

Goat CompanionGoatCompanion
A mountain goat guards you. It charges anything that comes near you and rams it -- and everything beside it -- flying away. Grows with the waves.
Now: A ram of 24 at wave 1 every 2.2s on whatever comes within 9.2 m of you; everything within 1 m of the target takes 40%. It charges at 4.6 m/s, trots at 4 m/s, and between rams ambles at 2.6 m/s, 1–2.2 m from you. Damage grows with the waves.
Its numbers above are read from the game. It was a spirit Wolf that hurled enemies into your orb's path until the player asked for something that kept them off instead: it rams whatever comes near you — always whatever is closest to you, switching mid-charge if something else gets nearer — and shoves the target and everything beside it hard, straight away from you. Between rams it never stands still, ambling from spot to spot around you and trotting to catch up if you outrun it. The player retuned it on 1–2 October to ram less often for less, with a smaller splash and a much slower charge; on 29 September it rammed for 44 every 1.2s, splashed 1.8 m for 70% and charged at 12.4 m/s. The daze is Concussion's alone, and the shove plays under a daze rather than after it. The benchmark figures from 29 September (+124% damage, +175% time survived) predate the retune: measured again on 3 October it added +7% damage and no survival at all, below a typical rare, so it was made a RARE without cutting its numbers further. Since the same day it walks round trees instead of through them, like the Demon.
