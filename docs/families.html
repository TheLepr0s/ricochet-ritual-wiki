Families

Twelve families. You pick one to start a run with, every card belongs to one, and holding enough of one switches its traits on.

Choosing a family

The last step before a run is a circle of the twelve families, like the one below. The run starts with one card from the family you pick — one of its weakest: the lowest rarity it has among the cards a fresh run can be dealt, so nothing that needs another card, a companion or an ability first. Where several share that rarity, one of them is picked at random.

The twelve families

Every card belongs to exactly one family. Each card you own in a family multiplies the draw weight of that family's other cards by +30%, up to 5 cards' worth (×2.5 at most). Rarity, the difficulty's odds and family all multiply together, so a family you lean into still keeps its legendaries rare. Your two leading families also name the build.

Traits

Hold 3, 5 and 7 (Frost and Void 3, 4 and 7; Kinetic 2, 4 and 6) different cards of one family and its three tiers switch on — a second copy of a card does not count: bronze, silver, and the gold capstone. They are never bought or picked: they follow your cards, and a card given up (Transmute, the Collector) takes its tier with it. In a run, the column on the right of the screen shows every family you hold and how far it is to the next tier, and hovering a row shows all three. A card that would complete a tier says so above it on the card screen.
