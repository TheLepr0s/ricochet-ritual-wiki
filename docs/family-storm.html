Shop boosts

Bought on Storm's panel in the Essence Shop, which shows your two most prominent families.

BoostEffect
IResonant FieldLightning and void pulses charge what they hit for 3 seconds: your orb deals 25% more damage to a charged enemy.
IIForked BoltStorm attacks chain to one more enemy: Chain Lightning jumps once more, and every other Storm hit arcs to the nearest enemy within 2.6 m for half its damage.
IIIThunderstruckA hit that takes 15% of an enemy's health calls down a bolt that stuns it for 1 second. Once every 3 seconds per enemy.
